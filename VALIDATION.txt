USER-REPORTED IN-GAME VALIDATION (development environment, main runtime Skyrim SE 1.6.1170; also launched on 1.7.104 — limited check, not thoroughly tested):
- Core features exercised in the author's environment: succubus awakening/leveling, Essence drain (single/concentration/area), charm & servantship tiers, Sweet Vassal (reanimated and living), H-Skills, Nightmare Embrace, sigils, Essence Shards, Sweet Elixirs, and the full MCM.
- Human-mode toggle preserves progress and restores on re-awakening.
- FOMOD language choice (English/Japanese) applies the correct translation.
- In-game bug-report generation writes ASTR2_BugReport.txt to the SKSE log folder.

LIMITS / UNVERIFIED:
- A native crash can occur if an enemy walks up and attacks after an OStim scene has started (scenes are guarded only at launch).
- Mixing an old OStim version can cause scene freezes; the tested version is 7.5.1b.
- Quest-critical NPCs are intentionally excluded from Sweet Vassal.
- Sweet Vassal (living) + a separate follower-management mod may cause a revived wild NPC (e.g. bandit) to reappear as an ally at respawn; cause not fully identified.
- Compatibility with arbitrary mod setups, body types other than 3BA, and runtimes other than 1.6.1170 are not exhaustively tested.

DATA:
- Persistent data (Records.json = lifetime records, Config.json = settings) lives in SKSE StorageUtilData (ASuccubusTaleR2 folder), external to game saves.
- Runtime/source archives exclude developer debug logs and diagnostics. Source retains licenses.
