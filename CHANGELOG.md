# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — First public release

ASuccubusTaleR2 (ASTR2) — an OStim-integrated succubus mod for Skyrim SE. Become a succubus: seduce NPCs and drain their Essence through OStim scenes to grow in power, charm victims into servants, and raise the dead as your vassals.

- Succubus leveling (Lv1–100) with auto-scaling max Magicka/Health/Stamina, regeneration, Speech, and skill-XP bonuses.
- Essence drain: single-target, concentration, and area/Ravenous drains, plus OStim-orgasm drain and scene finishers.
- Charm & Servantship with 5 tiers (Prey / Captive / Vassal / Thrall / Pet) and Servant Sync.
- Sweet Vassal: raise the dead as a reanimated thrall, or revive them to life (Lv9+).
- H-Skills, Nightmare Embrace, Sigils, Essence Shards, and Sweet Elixirs.
- Full MCM: feature toggles, power/cost/growth sliders, status, servant roster, sigil configuration, and an in-game bug-report generator.
- English / Japanese UI selected via the FOMOD installer.

Ships with a custom SKSE plugin (ASTR2SKSE). Developed and tested on Skyrim SE 1.6.1170. Distributed under GPL-3.0; source published on GitHub.
